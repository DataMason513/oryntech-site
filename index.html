Revive

Win back lost customers and rescue new leads, automatically.

Every business has two leaks: the customers who quietly stopped coming, and the enquiries nobody followed up. Revive plugs both. It reads your customer records, works out who can be contacted and how, and runs the follow-up for you.

  • Former customers get a short, personal win-back sequence with a real offer.
  • New enquiries get an instant reply and timed follow-ups, including a text back on missed calls.
  • Replies are read and answered. Anyone ready to come back is booked or handed to you.
  • Consent, opt-outs and sending windows are enforced on every message, so it stays compliant.

Runs inside your existing CRM. Priced per client, with a pilot first.

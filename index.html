Revive

Win back lost customers and rescue new leads, automatically.

Every local business has two leaks: the customers who quietly stopped coming, and the enquiries nobody answered in time. Revive plugs both. It reads your customer records, works out who may be contacted and how, runs the follow-up, and hands you the people who are ready to come back.

Two sequences, each with a fixed end. Days count from the trigger.

A customer who has gone quiet

  1. Day 0A short personal check-in from your business. No offer, no pressure.
  2. Day 3A reason to come back: what's new, what they were part of.
  3. Day 7One clear offer with a date on it.
  4. Day 12A last note, and the door stays open.
  5. ThenWe stop. Any reply pauses the sequence; STOP ends it on every channel.

A new enquiry or a missed call

  1. Minute 1An instant reply, or a text back on a missed call, in your name.
  2. Day 1 to 5Timed follow-ups until they answer or book.
  3. ThenWe stop. Anyone who replies gets a person, not another automation.

What Revive never does

  • Message anyone without a consent basis on record, and it checks the Canadian anti-spam rules before every send.
  • Contact a minor. Guardians only, always.
  • Keep going after a STOP, an unsubscribe or a do-not-contact flag, on any channel.
  • Send outside your quiet hours, or more than the daily cap you set.
  • Pretend to be a person. Messages go out in your name; replies are reviewed by one.
  • Use bought lists. It works only with people who already chose you once.

  • We report booked appointments and returned customers. Replies are not results.

Priced per location, with the pilot first. If it doesn't earn its keep, you stop at day thirty with nothing to unwind.

Questions owners ask first

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, it's a good reason to book the call.

Are we too small for this?

Probably not. The work pays off fastest in businesses of five to fifty people, where one owner or manager is still doing the follow-ups and the IT themselves. If you're smaller than that, the scoping call will say so and point you at the two or three things worth doing on your own.

What does it cost?

Managed IT is a fixed monthly fee per person. Automations and custom builds are quoted as a fixed price with a delivery date before any work starts. Revive is priced per location, with a paid thirty-day pilot first. You will see every number in writing before you commit to anything.

How long does a build take?

Most automations ship in weeks, not months. Larger custom builds are broken into stages you can use as they land. The quote carries the date, and you see progress weekly.

What does the AI actually do, and what does a person do?

The AI reads, drafts, classifies and routes: it writes the first draft of a reply, sorts the inbox, watches the monitors, fills in the report. A person approves anything that goes to a customer, changes your data or spends your money, and a person is who you talk to.

What do we own when you leave?

Everything. The code and its repository, the accounts and licences, the documentation, and the data. Access is handed over on the last day, and we walk your next provider through it if you want us to.

What if it doesn't work?

Pilots and first stages are scoped so you can stop at the end with nothing to unwind. Retainers are month to month. If we think something won't pay for itself, we say so before you spend on it.
